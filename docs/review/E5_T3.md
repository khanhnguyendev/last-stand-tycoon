## 2. What tier 3 adds

- **Map:** a fourth lane from the south-west. The tier-2 sign at the front lot reads "Buy the lot" and costs 1500. Buying it and winning the Baron night opens the lot, `tower_sw`, `fence_sw`, and moves the queue to the east side.
- **Enemies:** the siege brute (240 HP, 4x damage to fences, 8 steaks) and Baron von Hop, the tier-2 boss (500 HP, fast, 150 steaks).
- **Branches:** at tier 3 every max-level building offers two upgrades on stand-to-pay pads. Towers: Longbow (120 damage every 3.0 s, long range) or Volley (three targets). Fences: Stone wall (640 HP, halves brute damage) or Spike fence (hurts attackers and passing hares). The choice is permanent; a destroyed fence loses its branch.
- **Diner:** a second storey, shown by a five-step reveal that a tap skips after 0.6 s.
- **Saves:** schema 6; older saves load.

## 4. Difficulty: what real play showed, and the ruling (D-280)

The approved values were pressure 12 to 15 at tier 3 with brutes on both lanes. The tier bot played three real 32-day runs (seeds 20260930, 1, 2).

| Setting (threat policy, three seeds pooled) | Tier-3 nights needing a retry | Lowest diner on a held night | Median over held nights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approved (cap 15, brutes main + side) | 23 of 40 | 1% | 41% |
| Cap 13 | 8 of 40 | 1% | 58% |
| Cap 12 | 1 of 40 | 4% | 69% |
| **Cap 12, brutes on the main lane only (applied)** | **0 of 40** | 1% | 79%, 81% and 94% (per seed) |

- **Applied:** `tier_cap[3]` 15 to 12, `brute_cap_side[3]` 1 to 0.
- **Consequence:** tier 3 starts at its cap, so pressure no longer ramps during tier 3. What escalates is the brute count over the first three days, plus the fourth lane.
- **Weak spot:** on seed 1 the second and fourth tier-3 nights were held at 4% and 1% while the build was being completed. From the fifth tier-3 night on, the lowest across all seeds is 42%.
- **The Baron night** held with no retries on all three seeds (diner 52% to 68%). No Baron change was needed.
- **Why the first sims failed:** their saves were built from day-13 gear stamped as a later day. The Baron-night and cap-night fixtures (sims 2, 3, 6) now come from the bot's own run; the fixtures of sims 4, 5 and 7 are still constructed, which makes sim 5 conservative.
- **The 0 of 40 is for the threat policy.** Volley + Spike and Volley + Stone also need no retry; Longbow + Stone needs 1 of 40 and Longbow + Spike 6 of 40 (section 5).
- Raw: `media/e5t3/balance/` (`README.md` says which files are current; `study2/` holds the 24 round-1 runs, `applied/` the per-night data of the applied setting).

## 5. Branch policies (Task 24)

Three seeds, 32 days of real bot play each. Points are the mean diner HP at dawn over tier-3 nights, out of 100.

| | Stone wall | Spike fence |
|---|---|---|
| **Longbow** | 67.2 | 55.8 |
| **Volley** | 87.2 | 76.7 |

| Policy | Points | Retry nights | Fences lost per night | Gold on branches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volley + Stone (report only) | 87.2 | 0 | 1.07 | 6,700 |
| Volley + Spike (`all_b`) | 76.7 | 0 | 1.92 | 9,800 |
| Threat-matched (`threat`) | 75.4 | 0 | 1.22 | 7,200 |
| Longbow + Stone (`all_a`) | 67.2 | 1 | 1.17 | 6,800 |
| Longbow + Spike (`mixed`) | 55.8 | 6 | 1.86 | 9,500 |

- **Tower effect:** Volley beats Longbow by 20.4 points. **Fence effect:** Stone beats Spike by 10.9. They add up (interaction 1.0), and hold on all three seeds.
- **`DOMINANT_BRANCH: yes`** over the spec's four policies (gap 20.9 points; the threshold is 15), and **`DOMINANT_BRANCH_2x2: yes`** over the four fixed pairs (gap 31.4).
- **`THREAT_BEST: tie`.** Threat-matched against Volley + Spike differs by +8.4, -12.4 and +0.2 points per seed. Reading the telegraph does not beat a fixed choice. Against Volley + Spike it loses fewer fences and spends less; against Volley + Stone it is behind on every column.
- **Not retuned in this slice.** Which branch should win is your design call.
- **Both weak branches were tuned for pressure 15.** Longbow became 120 damage per 3.0 s so that it would kill the fewest hares when hares reach 73 HP; at the applied cap of 12 the last-wave hare has 51.7 HP, and the first proposal (80 per 1.8 s) would keep that ordering too. Spike fence's damage was meant to grow with the night's HP multiplier; with the cap equal to the base that factor is always 1.0.
- **Limits of the data:** three seeds; a retried night counts its final, easier attempt, which flatters only the two Longbow rows; "mixed" was defined by me (the spec named it without defining it).
- Raw: `media/e5t3/balance/policies.txt`.

## 9. Performance (measured once, at the end: D-260)

Profile web build of `32b4f95`, iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.5), fixture `tier3_cap_threat` (a tier-3 night, full build with branches), `export/perf_night3.sh`. The gate reading is the median of three runs (D-199).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 58.8 | 110 ms | 48 | 76% / 60% |
| 2 | 58.9 | 153 ms | 48 | 81% / 69% |
| 3 | 58.5 | 110 ms | 48 | 82% / 58% |
| **Median (the reading)** | **58.8** | **110 ms** | 48 | |
| Tier-2 night, same session (one run) | 59.4 | 110 ms | 43 | 78% / 65% |
| Reference: final review, tier-1 night 3 | 59.9 | 106 ms | | |

- **Tier 3 costs about 1 fps** against the tier-1 reference and 0.6 fps against a tier-2 night measured in the same session. Five more draw calls.
- **The worst-frame gate (under 60 ms) still fails**, as it did at the final review (known issue 2). In every tier-3 run the worst frame lands 0.1 to 0.2 s into the measured window, at its start.
- **Idle caveat:** every run started at 76% idle or more. Mid-run idle never reached 75% (58% to 71%), because that sample includes the Simulator itself; the final-review reading shows the same pattern. Two extra runs read 58.7 and 58.6 fps; the median of all five is 58.7 fps and 137 ms. One earlier run was discarded while macOS was indexing.
- Nothing was tuned. Raw: `media/e5t3/checkpoint/perf/perf.md`.
