What the numbers say, and what is only a hypothesis:
- **South-west is the weak tier-4 lane** (-8.9). Hypothesis: it is the lane the back lot could not give a second
  tower (D-314.24). Against it: with the same layout, round 1 at cap 15 had south-west at -4.9, and round 2 at cap
  14 has -10.0, so the cap change doubled the deficit. Not proven either way.
- **The Wrecker night beats the Volley builds,** and HP is probably not why. A "3 retries" night is three losses
  and a win at mercy 0.55, so the winning Wrecker has about 594 HP. Round 2's 25% HP cut moved no Volley run below
  3 retries, and round 2's second retry (756 HP) still lost where round 1's winning attempt faced 792 HP. The escort
  wave, or the Volley's brute weakness (x0.3; the Wrecker counts as a brute), is more likely. Winning at retry 2
  would need roughly -40% HP, if HP were the cause at all.
- **The tier-3 retry nights (seed 53, day 24) are not the bot saving for tier 4.** It held 13 and 153 gold at
  closeup, and the ladder was not done; saving shows on days 26 to 29. Cause unknown. One candidate: the uncapped
  bot has the tier-4 sign in its graph from tier 3's first day, so its walks differ.
- **The first tier-4 night is trivial for a full tier-3 build** (a median 100%). The conflict with the floor that
  phase 1 measured is inferred here, not measured: at tier 4 the floor has 26.7 points of headroom (51.7% against
  25%), and the brute caps were never tried.
