What the numbers say:
- **South-west is the weak lane at tier 4.** It is the lane the back lot could not give a second tower (D-314.24): no
  clear land reaches its fence at level-1 range. On a south-west siege seed the threat-matched build loses 7 to 11
  points to the best fixed pair.
- **The Wrecker beats the Volley builds.** It counts as a brute, and the Volley's weakness is brutes (×0.3), so a
  Volley build on the siege lane barely scratches it. Its HP is already at -25% (1440 to 1080).
- **The first tier-4 night is trivial for a full tier-3 build** (pressure 12, a median 100%).

## 2. What was built

- **The Wrecker**, the siege boss that ends tier 3:
  - HP 1080, 15 damage a second, fence damage 12 times a boar's, the Boar King's steaks;
  - it walks the siege lane;
  - grey stone slabs and a dark coat (`docs/review/media/e8/boss/`).
- **The back lot** (D-314.24): `tower_n2` covers north and `tower_e2` covers east, with the tier-4 sign at
  (13.0, -2.2).
  - Towers per lane at tier 4: west 2, north 3, east 3, south-west 1.
  - At tier 4 the east yard's kerb loses the pieces beside `tower_e2` (a visible change).
  - Shots: `docs/review/media/e8/backlot/`.
- **Tier-4 nights:**
  - two side lanes at once; the resting lane is drawn among the neutral lanes on a new stream, so tiers 1 to 3 draw
    as before;
  - waves at pressure 12 to 14 (cap 14 after round 2).
- **Saves:** schema 8; the v7 to v8 step adds nothing.
- **The reveal:** 4 steps (the north lot, `tower_n2`, the north-east strip, `tower_e2`), with the banner "The back
  lot opens!" and the warm-up prebuilding every step.
- **The bot:**
  - buys tier 4 after the tier-3 ladder; branches the new towers under every policy;
  - can be capped at a tier, which the tier-3 sims and the tier-3 baseline use.
- **The bench:** tier-4 rules are judged on sums over four seeds (D-314.26); all 11 pass at the tier-4 cap 14.
- **The report** reads tier 4: its own verdict block, the Wrecker night, set #6 at 45 days, a capped tier-3 check.
