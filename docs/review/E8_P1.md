## 2. What changed

- **Weaknesses** (spec 3.2):
  - Longbow deals 0.3 times its damage to hares, so most hares take two arrows.
  - Volley deals 0.3 times its damage to brutes.
  - A Spike fence takes 4 times a brute's hit.
  - The Stone wall has no weakness. Its off-lane cost is the gold not spent on a Spike fence (D-314.4).
- **Kind classes:** the Baron counts as a hare for these multipliers (D-314.10). There is no Longbow before tier 3,
  so tier 2 play is unchanged.
- **The bench's rules:** E6's "paying never makes it worse" is replaced.
  - ON: the specialist is clearly best on its own lane.
  - OFF: the Longbow, the Volley and the Spike fence are clearly worse than unbranched on the wrong lane.
  - NEUTRAL: no branch is much worse than unbranched on a neutral lane.
  - The 11 rules pass at pressures 10 and 12, and every rule's mutation was seen to fail it.
  - The replaced-test table is at the top of `tests/bench/test_branch_bench_full.gd`.
- **The branch card** gains a fourth line naming the weakness (section 3).
- **The report:**
  - FIRST_NIGHT_BAND is new.
  - FIXED_SPREAD becomes information, since the builds should now differ.
  - Measurement sets #5 and #6 come from the scan.
- **Tier 3 still starts at pressure 10.** Round 2's 11 was reverted (D-314.21).
