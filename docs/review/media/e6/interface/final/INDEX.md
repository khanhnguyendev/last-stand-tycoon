## Defects seen

Nothing was fixed in this task. Each line is a defect seen in the images above (full size unless it says 40%).

1. `marker_sw_hare`: the south-west hare badge is cut by the bottom screen edge (half a rabbit); `marker_sw_siege`: the badge sits 20 px from the left edge on top of the "dev" watermark. The south-west count row is in the bottom 15% of the screen.
2. `marker_west_siege`, `marker_east_hare`, `marker_north_hare`: the other lane's badge or count row is cut by the left or right screen edge.
3. `lane_line_pick`, `lane_line_siege`, `lane_line_hare`: the west siege badge overlaps the "3000" gold number and sits against the heart; the north hare badge overlaps the diner chimney.
4. `reveal_badges`: the two badges are about 45 px wide (18 px at 40%): they read only as a hammer mark and a rabbit blob.
5. `arrows_three`, `arrows_three_zone`: the third (south-west) arrow and the brute mark on the east arrow are 10 to 18 px at 40%: they do not read.
6. `far_stage`: at HOME itself fence_sw pad b is 1.4 m away, so it is in its NEAR stage there: the FAR view of the yard cannot be taken from HOME (the tool now keeps the camera at HOME and moves the hero to (3.0, 5.5)); the hero's resting place shows a pad cost.
7. `near_pad_tower_nw_a`, `near_pad_tower_nw_b`: the hero and the pad costs stand behind the diner's see-through roof and are washed out; the 500s do not read at 40%.
8. `hero_front`: the cost is half covered by the hero's body ("5?0"): intended depth order, but the number cannot be read.
9. `near_pad_fence_w_a`, `near_pad_fence_w_b`, `unarmed`: the glyph and cost touch the hero's head and hair.
10. `near_pad_tower_ne_a`, `near_pad_tower_e_b`, `near_pad_fence_e_b`, `far_stage`, `unarmed`, `hero_front`, `tether_tower_e`, `tethered`: the station labels "Counter 30" and "Freezer 25" draw their name and price over each other; in `near_pad_tower_sw_b` "Counter" is cut by the right edge.
11. `near_pad_tower_e_b`: the "0" tip counter label touches the Longbow pad's ring.
12. `near_pad_fence_n_a`, `near_pad_fence_n_b`: the hero stands on the stone wall and the tower pads below him are half behind the diner roof.
13. `pips_far`: the three level stars are 3 px dots at 40%.
14. `tether_tower_e`, `tethered`: the tether dash is 8 px at 40%; with the hero on it, it is hidden by his body.
15. The "dev" watermark shows at the bottom-left of every shot (debug build marker), over the south-west badge in `marker_sw_siege`.
16. The `near_pad_*` views were taken with the hero off every pad on an offset chosen by the tool (south, then west or east of the pad); for tower_sw the pad that shows its cost is not the one nearest the hero's feet on screen, so "pad a" reads as the neighbour pad (`near_pad_tower_sw_a`).
