(S1) and the S2 hero cards and guards are **never cut**. S3 save/mercy is not on this list; cutting it
needs its own decision.

The days saved are rough estimates against a full version of each sub-project and get refined in
each sub-project's plan. Rank 1 is the cut to take first: the most days for the least risk to the
playtest gate.
